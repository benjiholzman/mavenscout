Terms of Reference — Senior Monitoring & Evaluation Consultant, Sahel Resilience Program

Background
Horizon Development Fund is supporting a five-year climate adaptation and food security program across the Sahel, working with farmer cooperatives, women's groups and local government in northern Nigeria, Niger and Burkina Faso. The program combines drought-tolerant agriculture, community early-warning systems, and cash-based social protection for the most vulnerable households.

Scope of work
The consultant will design and lead the program's monitoring and evaluation system. Tasks include: developing the results framework and indicator reference sheets; designing the baseline household survey (survey design, sampling, questionnaire development and enumerator training); leading a mixed-methods impact evaluation with a quasi-experimental design; building a gender-sensitive monitoring approach in line with the program's gender mainstreaming strategy; and training local M&E staff through workshop facilitation and mentoring.

Deliverables
1. Monitoring and evaluation framework and MEAL plan.
2. Baseline survey report with statistical analysis of household resilience indicators.
3. Impact evaluation design and mid-term evaluation report.
4. Data management protocols and dashboards.

Qualifications
- At least 10 years of experience in monitoring and evaluation of climate adaptation, food security or rural development programs, preferably in West Africa.
- Proven experience with impact evaluation and survey design; strong statistical analysis skills (Stata, R or Python).
- Experience with gender mainstreaming in M&E systems.
- Fluency in French and English is required; Hausa is an asset.
- Based in Abuja or Accra preferred; willing to travel across the Sahel.
- Experience with FCDO, USAID or World Bank-funded programs is an advantage.
