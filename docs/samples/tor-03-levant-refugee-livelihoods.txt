Terms of Reference — Livelihoods and Social Cohesion Assessment, Syrian Refugees and Host Communities in Jordan and Lebanon

Background
More than a decade into the Syria crisis, refugee and host communities in Jordan and Lebanon face shrinking humanitarian funding, high unemployment and rising tensions over jobs, rent and services. A consortium of international NGOs, working with UNHCR and municipal authorities, is designing the next phase of a livelihoods programme that supports home-based businesses, micro and small enterprises, and work permits for refugees, alongside activities to strengthen social cohesion between refugee and host communities.

Purpose
The assessment will inform programme design by identifying viable livelihood pathways for refugees and vulnerable host-community members, especially women and youth, and by analysing the drivers of tension and cohesion in the target municipalities.

Scope of work
- Conduct a market assessment in six municipalities (Irbid, Mafraq and Zarqa in Jordan; Tripoli, Akkar and Bekaa in Lebanon) covering labour demand, value chains with growth potential, and constraints facing home-based and small enterprises.
- Assess the regulatory environment for refugee employment and business registration, including work permit sectors and municipal licensing.
- Design and run a household survey (approximately 1,500 households) and qualitative research (focus groups and key informant interviews) on livelihoods, debt and inter-community relations.
- Analyse social cohesion dynamics: perceptions of competition, trust in local institutions, and the role of programme activities in reducing or aggravating tension.
- Recommend SME support models (grants, business development services, access to finance) and conflict-sensitive targeting approaches.

Deliverables
1. Inception report with research tools.
2. Market assessment report for each country.
3. Social cohesion analysis with conflict-sensitivity recommendations.
4. Final synthesis report and presentation to the consortium and UNHCR.

Qualifications
- At least 8 years of experience in refugee livelihoods, social cohesion or economic recovery programming in the Middle East.
- Proven experience with market research and mixed-methods survey design; strong qualitative research skills.
- Fluency in Arabic is required; excellent written English.
- Prior work with UNHCR or refugee-response consortia is an asset.
- Based in Jordan or Lebanon preferred, with the ability to travel to all six municipalities.
