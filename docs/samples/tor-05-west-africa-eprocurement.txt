Termes de Référence / Terms of Reference — Design and Implementation Support for an E-Procurement and Digital Public Services Platform, Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire

Background
The governments of Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire are modernizing public procurement as part of wider digital governance reforms supported by a multilateral development bank. Both countries have adopted e-procurement strategies but still run most tenders on paper, with fragmented supplier registries and limited interoperability between procurement, treasury and tax systems. The programme also funds a shared digital public services layer (identity verification, e-payments and document exchange) that the e-procurement platform must use.

Objective
A firm is sought to provide technical design and implementation support for the national e-procurement platforms in both countries and to integrate them with the digital public services layer.

Scope of work
- Review the current public procurement process and legal framework, and define functional and non-functional requirements for e-procurement (e-tendering, e-evaluation, e-contract management, supplier registry).
- Prepare the solution architecture: cloud architecture options (government data centre vs. sovereign cloud), database management, interoperability with treasury and tax systems, and cybersecurity requirements.
- Support procurement of the software platform and oversee web development and configuration by the selected vendor.
- Lead change management for procurement officers and suppliers, including a training of trainers programme and user support materials in French.
- Design open data publication of procurement information to support transparency and anti-corruption objectives.

Deliverables
1. Diagnostic and requirements report.
2. Solution architecture and cybersecurity plan.
3. Platform go-live in two pilot ministries per country.
4. Change management and training plan; trained national trainers.

Qualifications
- A consulting firm (small or large) with at least 10 years of experience in digital governance and e-procurement in Francophone Africa.
- Key experts in public procurement, solution architecture, database management and web development, and change management.
- Working language is French; all deliverables in French with English summaries. Fluency in French is required for all key experts.
- Presence in Dakar or Abidjan is an advantage.
