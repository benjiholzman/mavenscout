Terms of Reference — Market Assessment and Product Design for Smallholder Agricultural Finance, Philippines and Indonesia

Background
A regional agricultural finance facility, financed by a multilateral development bank with co-financing from IFAD, aims to expand access to credit, insurance and digital payments for smallholder farmers and agri-SMEs in Mindanao (Philippines) and Eastern Java and Nusa Tenggara (Indonesia). Partner financial institutions include rural banks, cooperatives and microfinance institutions that have limited experience lending against seasonal cash flows.

Objective
To assess demand for and supply of agricultural finance among smallholders and agri-SMEs in priority value chains (coconut, coffee, seaweed and maize), and to design three to four financial products that partner institutions can pilot.

Scope of work
- Conduct a market research study combining a demand-side survey of 1,200 farm households and agri-SMEs with supply-side interviews of partner financial institutions.
- Map value chains and the financing gaps at each node, including input suppliers, aggregators, processors and buyers.
- Analyse the regulatory and investment climate for agricultural lending and digital financial services in both countries.
- Design financial products (e.g., seasonal loans, warehouse receipt financing, value-chain finance through off-takers) with financial modeling of costs, risk and portfolio performance for each partner institution.
- Integrate gender analysis so products address barriers faced by women farmers and women-led enterprises.
- Prepare a pilot plan with indicators for monitoring financial inclusion outcomes.

Deliverables
1. Inception report and survey instruments.
2. Market research report covering demand, supply and value-chain financing gaps.
3. Product design notes with financial models for each partner institution.
4. Pilot implementation and monitoring plan.

Qualifications
- At least 10 years of experience in agricultural finance, financial inclusion or agribusiness development in Southeast Asia.
- Demonstrated skills in market research, survey design and financial modeling.
- Experience with rural development programmes financed by ADB, IFAD or the World Bank is an advantage.
- Knowledge of Filipino or Indonesian is an asset.
- Based in the Philippines or Indonesia preferred.
